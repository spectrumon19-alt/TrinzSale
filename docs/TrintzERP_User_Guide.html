Section 2

Getting Started & Login

Accessing the application

Open a modern web browser (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge recommended) and go to the address provided for your installation — for an on-site setup this is typically http://localhost:5000, or the URL/IP given by your administrator.

Logging in

  1. Enter your username and password on the login screen.
  2. If Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is enabled for your account, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (e.g. Google Authenticator).
  3. On success you are taken to the Dashboard (or to billing, depending on your role).
First-time setup Default accounts (such as an initial administrator) must have their passwords changed immediately after first login. Your administrator will provide your personal credentials.

License activation

TrintzERP is a licensed product. After login the system automatically validates your license. If a valid license is not found, you are guided to the License Activation screen — enter the license key provided with your purchase to unlock full access. Once activated you are returned to the Dashboard automatically.

Tip — staying signed in securely Always use Logout when leaving the counter, especially on shared machines. Sessions are protected by secure tokens and will expire for safety.
Section 3

User Roles & Permissions

TrintzERP uses role-based access control backed by a fine-grained, screen-level permission system. Every user is assigned one role; administrators can then further allow or restrict individual screens per user.

RoleTypical userAccess
Super AdminBusiness owner / system owner Full, unrestricted access to every module including administration, permissions, backups and AI settings.
AdminStore manager / senior staff Broad operational access (sales, purchases, inventory, reports, customer/supplier credit, cancellations) subject to granted permissions.
ManagerShift / department lead Operational access to assigned areas; typically reporting and day-to-day management functions.
CashierCounter staff Focused on billing and sales; restricted from administrative and financial-control screens.

Screen-level permissions

Beyond the role, Super Admins can grant or revoke access to individual screens (e.g. Reports, Token Usage, Credit Management) for each user from the Administration → Access Permissions panel. The system follows a deny-by-default policy: a user only sees a screen if their role or an explicit permission allows it.

How it protects you Access is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the menu. Even a direct attempt to open a restricted page is blocked and redirected to an Access Denied screen.

Section 5

Sales & Billing

The Sales screen is the heart of TrintzERP — designed for fast, accurate counter billing with automatic GST calculation and multiple payment modes.

Creating a sale — step by step

  1. Find products: search by name or SKU and add them to the cart.
  2. Set quantity & discount: adjust quantity and apply line or invoice-level discounts.
  3. GST is automatic: CGST/SGST is calculated per product from its GST rate.
  4. Add customer (optional): capture name and mobile for receipts and credit.
  5. Choose payment mode: Cash, UPI/Online (with transaction ID) or Credit.
  6. Complete sale: the invoice is generated with a unique number; an e-Invoice IRN and QR are attached automatically when enabled.
  7. Print/share receipt: print or email the receipt to the customer.
Figure 1 — Sales & Billing flow: cart → GST → payment mode → invoice (with IRN) → receipt. Credit sales automatically raise the customer’s receivable.
Credit sales & the ledger When a sale is billed as Credit, TrintzERP automatically records the amount against the matched customer’s account (a Tally-style receivable). Cancelling such an invoice reverses the entry automatically, keeping your books in sync.

Stock safety

The system checks stock availability before completing a sale and decrements inventory as items are sold, preventing overselling.

Section 8

Credit Management — Receivables & Payables

TrintzERP provides a unified, accounting-grade view of money owed to you (customer receivables) and money owed by you (supplier payables), following standard Indian (Tally) conventions.

👥 Customer Receivables

Track per-customer outstanding balances. A credit sale / charge increases what a customer owes you; a payment received reduces it. View statements, ageing and transaction history per customer.

🚚 Supplier Payables

Track what you owe each supplier, record payments, and see advances. Outstanding amounts roll up into the credit dashboard.

The Credit Dashboard

A single overview shows total Receivables (To Collect), total Payables (To Pay), and your Net Position. Customer advances are reported separately so they are never confused with money owed to you.

TermPlain meaning
ReceivableMoney a customer still has to pay you.
PayableMoney your business still has to pay a supplier.
AdvanceA customer has paid more than due / you paid a supplier ahead.
Net PositionTotal receivables minus total payables.
Always in sync Credit sales, payments, returns and cancellations all post consistently to the ledger, and an automatic reconciliation flags any account whose stored balance drifts from its transaction history.

Ageing & statements

Outstanding amounts are bucketed by age (how overdue they are) using a first-in-first-out settlement model, and per-account statements can be exported to Excel for follow-up.

Section 9

GST Reports & Tally Export

GST compliance

Every invoice is GST-aware: CGST and SGST are computed per item from the product’s GST rate and HSN code. The GST Reports module summarises outward tax (on sales), input tax credit (ITC on purchases), and the net tax payable for a chosen period — the figures you need for return filing.

e-Invoice (IRN & QR)

When enabled, qualifying sales automatically receive an Invoice Reference Number (IRN) and a signed QR code, attached to the invoice for statutory e-invoicing.

Tally export

Export your sales, purchases and returns as Tally-compatible vouchers (XML). The export uses correct double-entry: credit sales debit the customer (Sundry Debtor); purchases credit the supplier (Sundry Creditor); returns post as Credit Notes — so your accountant can import directly into Tally.

Accountant-friendly GST summaries and Tally export are designed so your CA or accounts team can reconcile and file without re-keying data.
Section 10

Returns & Invoice Cancellation

Sales returns

Process customer returns against an original invoice. Returns restore stock, adjust GST correctly, and record the refund method — and they flow into reports and Tally export as Credit Notes.

Invoice cancellation

Authorised users (typically Admin/Super Admin) can cancel an invoice. On cancellation the system:

  • Restores the sold stock back to inventory;
  • Marks the invoice as Cancelled with an audit trail (who, when, reason);
  • Automatically reverses any credit-ledger entry created by that sale, restoring the customer’s receivable.
Controlled action Cancellation is permission-controlled and fully audited to prevent misuse.

AI Assistant

TrintzERP includes a built-in AI Assistant that lets you ask questions about your business in plain English — for example, “Which customers owe us the most?” or “What were today’s total sales?” — and receive instant, data-driven answers.

How it works (safely)

Your question is sent to the AI, which translates it into a read-only database query, runs it against your own data, and returns a clear answer. The assistant is restricted to safe, read-only lookups — it cannot modify, delete or expose protected information.

Figure 2 — AI Assistant: questions are converted to safe, read-only queries over your own data; usage is metered.

Technical Architecture

This section is intended for IT and technical staff. TrintzERP is a three-tier web application: a browser-based front end, a Python/Flask application server, and a PostgreSQL database, integrated with a small number of external services.

Figure 3 — System architecture: browser UI → Flask application server → PostgreSQL, with AI, backup, email and e-Invoice integrations.

  • Cloud / hosted: deployed to a cloud host (e.g. Render) and accessed via a URL.

Section 19

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection to use TrintzERP?
For an on-premise installation, day-to-day billing and management work on your local machine/LAN without internet. Internet is needed only for features that use external services — the AI assistant, Google Drive backup, email and GST e-Invoice (IRN) generation.
Which web browser should I use?
Any modern browser works; Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge are recommended for the best experience. The interface is also responsive for tablets.
How is GST calculated?
Each product carries a GST rate and HSN code. At billing, the system automatically splits tax into CGST and SGST per item, and the GST Reports module rolls these up for your return period.
What does a negative customer balance mean?
Following Tally convention, a negative balance means the customer owes you money (a receivable). A positive balance means the customer is in advance/credit. The screens label these clearly as “Receivable” and “Advance”.
What happens to stock and credit when I cancel an invoice?
Cancellation restores the sold stock to inventory and automatically reverses any credit ledger entry created by that sale, so both inventory and receivables stay correct. The action is audited.
Is the AI assistant safe with my data?
Yes. The assistant is limited to safe, read-only queries against your own database and cannot change or delete data. Administrators can configure it and monitor usage via Token Usage.
Can multiple staff use it at once?
Yes. Each user logs in with their own account and role; permissions control what each person can see and do.
How do I recover if the computer fails?
Restore from your most recent backup. With scheduled local + Google Drive backups enabled, an off-site copy is available. Test a restore periodically so you’re confident in recovery.
Can my accountant work with this?
Yes — GST summaries export for filing, and sales/purchases/returns export as Tally-compatible vouchers for direct import.
I forgot my password — what do I do?
Ask a Super Admin/Admin to reset it from User Management. For security, only administrators can reset passwords.
Why can’t I see a particular menu/screen?
Access is permission-controlled. If you need a screen that isn’t visible, ask your administrator to grant it from Access Permissions.
