Getting Started & Login

Sign in, activate your license and get to the counter quickly.

Logging in

  1. Open a modern browser (Chrome/Edge) and go to your TrintzERP address (e.g. http://localhost:5000).
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. If 2FA is enabled, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

License activation

After login the system validates your license automatically. If no valid license is found, you'll be guided to the License Activation screen — enter your key to unlock full access, then you'll return to the Dashboard.

First-time setup Change default passwords immediately after first login. Your administrator provides personal credentials.
Stay secure Always use Logout when leaving the counter on shared machines.

Sales & Billing

Fast counter billing with automatic GST and multiple payment modes.

Create a sale — step by step

  1. Find products — search by name or SKU and add to the cart.
  2. Set quantity & discount — line or invoice level.
  3. GST is automatic — CGST/SGST per product rate.
  4. Add customer (optional) — name & mobile for receipts and credit.
  5. Choose payment — Cash, UPI/Online (with txn ID), or Credit.
  6. Complete — invoice generated; e-Invoice IRN & QR attached when enabled.
  7. Print / email the receipt.
Credit sales & the ledger A sale billed as Credit automatically raises the matched customer's receivable. Cancelling such an invoice reverses it — keeping your books in sync.

GST Reports & Tally Export

Stay compliant and hand clean data to your accountant.

GST compliance

CGST/SGST are computed per item from the product's GST rate and HSN. GST Reports summarise outward tax, input tax credit (ITC) and the net tax payable for a period.

e-Invoice (IRN & QR)

When enabled, qualifying sales receive an IRN and signed QR code attached to the invoice.

Tally export

Export sales, purchases and returns as Tally-compatible vouchers (XML) with correct double-entry — credit sales debit the customer (Sundry Debtor), purchases credit the supplier (Sundry Creditor), returns post as Credit Notes.

Returns & Invoice Cancellation

Handle returns and cancellations without breaking your books.

Sales returns

Process returns against an original invoice — stock is restored, GST adjusted, refund method recorded, and the return flows into reports & Tally as a Credit Note.

Invoice cancellation

  • Restores sold stock to inventory;
  • Marks the invoice Cancelled with an audit trail (who, when, reason);
  • Auto-reverses any credit-ledger entry from that sale.
Controlled action Cancellation is permission-controlled and fully audited.

AI Assistant

Ask business questions in plain English; get answers from your own data.

Your question is converted into a read-only query, run against your data, and answered — for example, “Which customers owe us the most?” The assistant cannot modify or delete data.

AI Settings configure the assistant and Token Usage tracks consumption (both permission-controlled). A built-in Knowledge Base stores reference articles.

Admin Panel — Every Tile Explained

The Admin Panel is the single entry point for all administrative tools, grouped into three sections: Management, Data Tools and Configuration. A live stats bar at the top shows Products, Suppliers and Users counts.

How tile visibility works Each tile appears only if the user both (1) meets the tile's minimum role rank — L2 Manager+ L3 Admin+ L4 Super Admin only — and (2) has the tile's screen permission granted. This is deny-by-default, and it is enforced on the server, not just hidden from the menu.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Do I need internet to use TrintzERP?
For an on-premise install, billing and management work on your local machine/LAN without internet. Internet is needed only for external features — AI assistant, Google Drive backup, email and GST e-Invoice (IRN).
Which browser should I use?
Any modern browser; Chrome or Edge are recommended. The interface is responsive for tablets too.
How is GST calculated?
Each product carries a GST rate and HSN. At billing, tax is split into CGST and SGST per item, and GST Reports roll these up for your return period.
What does a negative customer balance mean?
Following Tally convention, a negative balance means the customer owes you (a receivable); a positive balance means the customer is in advance/credit. Screens label these clearly as “Receivable” and “Advance”.
What happens to stock and credit when I cancel an invoice?
Cancellation restores sold stock and automatically reverses any credit-ledger entry from that sale, keeping inventory and receivables correct. The action is audited.
Is the AI assistant safe with my data?
Yes — it is limited to safe, read-only queries against your own database and cannot change or delete data. Admins can configure it and monitor usage via Token Usage.
Can multiple staff use it at once?
Yes. Each user logs in with their own account and role; permissions control what each person can see and do.
How do I recover if the computer fails?
Restore from your most recent backup. With scheduled local + Google Drive backups, an off-site copy is available. Test a restore periodically.
Can my accountant work with this?
Yes — GST summaries export for filing, and sales/purchases/returns export as Tally-compatible vouchers.
I forgot my password — what now?
Ask a Super Admin/Admin to reset it from User Management. For security, only administrators can reset passwords.
Why can't I see a particular menu/screen?
Access is permission-controlled. Ask your administrator to grant it from Access Permissions.
